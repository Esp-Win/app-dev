# Straw (2025)

## Overview

**Straw** is a 2025 American psychological crime drama film written, produced, and directed by Tyler Perry. The film follows a struggling single mother whose life spirals out of control after a series of devastating events push her to her breaking point. It was released on Netflix on June 6, 2025.

## Plot Summary

The story centers on **Janiyah Wiltkinson**, a single mother struggling to care for her daughter while dealing with financial hardship, employment issues, and mounting personal challenges.

As one devastating event follows another, Janiyah is pushed to her emotional limits. A series of difficult circumstances forces her into desperate situations, creating a tense and emotional story that explores poverty, resilience, society's treatment of vulnerable individuals, and the consequences of overwhelming stress.
